I have joined [Artanim](https://artanim.ch/) to work on markerless motion capture.
This will probably need real-time deep-learning, many cameras and big GPUs.

Note: I have been following [Miralab](https://miralab.com/) research for years, probably because of a [1991 Eurographics Eiffel tower award](https://histoire3d.siggraph.org/index.php/Le_Pantin) [I was playing with as a kid](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-4-431-66890-9). The Artanim research lab was founded by Miralab's alumnis.
